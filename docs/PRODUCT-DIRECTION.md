# Product direction

Sigma is public tooling for ingesting sources and making sense of them. Podcasts came first, then X. The site is a showcase of that tooling and the owner's view of how to use it best: a Signals desk from public podcasts and X, and posts. Most work is a request to ingest named sources and report on a topic; the daily and weekly editions continue beside it.

A signal must be relevant, actionable and news-focused. It must work for a reader and for an agent: source links, attribution and timestamps belong beside the claim, and structured output must agree with the page.

Posts are opinions and articles curated with the owner. They draw on what Sigma ingested, attribute each sourced claim, and do not duplicate the owner's private research. Posts are opt-in: a private draft is never a public post merely because the builder can find it.

The code must be usable with someone else's sources. Operator inputs and editorial history belong outside the public repository. No product behavior may depend on the maintainer's accounts or storage layout.
