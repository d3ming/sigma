# Product direction

Sigma has two jobs: find signals for the US stock market and publish a Signals desk from public podcasts and X, and explain research methods and tooling through public research posts.

A signal must be relevant, actionable and news-focused. It must work for a reader and for an agent: source links, attribution and timestamps belong beside the claim, and structured output must agree with the page.

Research posts explain how a method works and what its evidence supports. They do not publish investment research, fair values, positions or trading calls. Posts are opt-in: a private draft is never a public post merely because the builder can find it.

The code must be usable with someone else's sources. Operator inputs and editorial history belong outside the public repository. No product behavior may depend on the maintainer's accounts or storage layout.
